# Desbank Protocol: Institutional RWA & Smart Liquidity

**EmergentSoft · Historical product concept**

> **Status:** Concept / historical prototype. This document is not a description of a live production protocol or currently available financial service.

Desbank is the proposed workflow and integration layer in the GreenLedger / Desbank product family. GreenLedger is the asset-evidence and tokenization-control layer; Desbank may provide customer-facing financial workflows after the relevant requirements, security controls and legal perimeter have been defined.

## Proposed product direction

Potential modules for future discovery and implementation:

- asset onboarding and case management;
- evidence references and provenance;
- policy-driven approvals and operational controls;
- portfolio and exposure reporting;
- integration adapters for institutional systems.

These are roadmap candidates, not currently available capabilities.

## Current evidence boundary

The repository does not substantiate active collateralized liquidity, lending, deposits, yield generation, production vaults, exchange, redemption, custody, bank integrations, mainnet deployment, regulatory approval or an independent security audit.

Earlier concept language referenced Sentinel, Aether OS, EmergentVaults, YieldOptimizer, BurnController, 3D scanning, a 42 ms execution target and historical launch dates. Those references are not verified product capabilities, performance benchmarks or current commitments and must not be used as such.

## Product architecture

- **GreenLedger:** proposed asset-evidence, attestation and tokenization-control layer.
- **Desbank:** proposed workflow and integration layer.
- **External systems:** customer identity, document management, asset registries and reporting systems, subject to integration discovery and approval.

## Release gates

Any future financial functionality such as lending, collateralization, deposits, yield, exchange, redemption, custody or liquidity routing requires a separate product specification, risk model, threat model, legal analysis, implementation, tests and explicit release approval.

The initial commercial proposition should be enterprise software and implementation services for controlled RWA workflows, not a promise of financial returns or liquidity.
