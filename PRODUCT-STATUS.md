# Desbank Protocol — Product Status and Repositioning

**Status:** historical concept repository; not a canonical active implementation.

## Role in the product family

Desbank is the proposed financial-workflow and integration layer in the GreenLedger / Desbank product family. GreenLedger is the asset-evidence and tokenization-control layer. The current canonical EVM implementation tracks are maintained separately:

- Ethereum / Base: https://github.com/alejandrolamas-netizen/Greeenledger-Desbank-eth
- Arbitrum: https://github.com/alejandrolamas-netizen/GreenLedger-Desbank-Arbitrum

This repository is retained for historical context and product evolution. Its current files do not demonstrate a complete protocol implementation.

## Claims removed from active positioning

The historical concept text mentioned real-time vault monitoring, collateralized liquidity, yield optimization, token burns, 3D asset scanning, institutional compliance, mainnet launches and an exit strategy. Those claims are not substantiated by the current repository contents and must not be used as current product capabilities or commitments.

In particular:
- no active liquidity venue or lending market is evidenced;
- no production vault or yield engine is evidenced;
- no bank, exchange or custodian integration is evidenced;
- no mainnet deployment is evidenced by this repository;
- no audit, regulatory approval or legal suitability is established;
- no 42 ms execution or latency benchmark is substantiated here.

## Proposed future scope — subject to discovery and approval

Desbank may evolve into a workflow and integration layer for:
1. asset onboarding and case management;
2. evidence and provenance references;
3. policy-based approvals and operational controls;
4. portfolio/exposure reporting;
5. integration adapters for customer systems.

Any future financial functionality (lending, collateral, deposits, yield, exchange, redemption, custody or liquidity routing) requires a distinct technical design, risk model, security review, legal analysis and explicit release approval.

## Product release rule

Do not present Desbank as a functioning financial protocol until the relevant modules are implemented, tested, independently reviewed where appropriate, deployed with verifiable evidence and cleared for the intended jurisdiction and use case.
